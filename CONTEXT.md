# Diction

Diction is an iOS app that plays interactive fiction (Z-machine and Glulx) with spoken narration and voice commands. This glossary covers the paywall vocabulary; other areas are added as they are modelled.

## Paywall

**Paywall**:
The single one-time purchase that gates the voice features, and the surfaces that sell it. An internal term: user-facing copy says "Unlock".
_Avoid_: unlock (as a noun, in docs and code), full version, IAP, premium

**Free** / **Unlocked**:
The two states of the app: without the paywall purchase, and with it. "Unlock"/"unlocked" are the user-facing words.
_Avoid_: demo, full, purchased, paid

**Locked**:
A feature the free state does not get.

**Voice commands**:
Issuing game and app commands by speaking instead of typing. A locked feature, except where free to try.
_Avoid_: speak-to-command, voice input, mic, "play using my voice" (a UI label, not the term)

**Neural narration**:
Narration through one of the neural (Kokoro) voices. A locked feature.
_Avoid_: premium voice, neural voice (that is one voice, not the feature), Kokoro (in user-facing text)

**Accessibility voice**:
Narration through one of Apple's on-board voices. Always free.
_Avoid_: system voice, Apple voice, TTS

**Bundled game**:
A game shipped inside the app and listed in the library. One today: All Things Devours.
_Avoid_: built-in, included, demo game

**Free-to-try voice commands**:
The one exception to the paywall: voice commands work in a bundled game without the purchase, with no limit on turns or time.
_Avoid_: demo, trial, showcase, exception
